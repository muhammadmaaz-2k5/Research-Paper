## 🧭 The Winning 1-Minute Pitch Formula
When speaking to Chinese professors or admissions panels, follow the **3-Part Structure**:
1. **The Hook (15s)**: Who you are + your superpower (4+ years shipping production distributed systems).
2. **The Problem & Solution (30s)**: The specific technical problem in their domain and your proposed solution.
3. **The Value to Their Lab (15s)**: Why you save them time and money (zero onboarding time, immediate code output).
